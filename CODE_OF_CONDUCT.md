# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community regardless of age,
body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality,
appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

Use welcoming language, respect different viewpoints, accept constructive
feedback, focus on what benefits the community, and show empathy. Harassment,
personal attacks, sexualized attention, doxxing, and other conduct reasonably
considered inappropriate are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile.
Maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation. Reports will be handled as confidentially
as practical.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
